// ABOUT

Two pillars: education & gamification

I'm a generalist by design — equally comfortable managing a dev team, sketching a UI, writing C#, or reading a spreadsheet. That range comes from spending my studies at the intersection of human-technology interaction and game design, then spending four self-directed years actually shipping games to figure out how every part of the pipeline fits together.

I approach every project with a gamified but critical eye: user-centered, playful in execution, but held to the same bar as any serious product. Whether it's a rehab tool for children with cerebral palsy or an internal course for electrical engineers, the question is the same — does it actually work for the person using it, and can it reach them at scale?

Today that plays out across three roles: building client gamification products as CEO of UniGames B.V., leading 40+ people as Team Lead of Totem Game Dev, and modeling the TU/e campus in Unity as a 3D designer for the university's autonomous-driving research lab.
